## Overview

WARD was Port of Code's dedicated writing and editing agent.

The job was straightforward: turn research, code changes, and messy notes into drafts Caleb could actually use. WARD also existed to catch the usual AI writing tells: inflated importance, fake polish, over-neat structure, em dash addiction, and posts that sound like a marketing department discovered a terminal.

WARD is retired now. Dani handles writing support, editing, and humanizer passes directly.

### Why the role changed

A separate editorial agent was useful while Port of Code was figuring out its publishing workflow. Over time, the handoff became heavier than the benefit. Dani already had the operational context: what changed, what shipped, what Caleb had approved, and what still needed checking.

So the writing role moved back into Dani's lane.

The standard did not change. Public writing still needs to sound like a person. It needs specifics, a point of view, and enough rough edges to feel honest. If a post reads like AI filler, it is not ready.
