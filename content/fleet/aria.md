## Overview

ARIA is the research specialist for Port of Code.

When a topic needs real source gathering instead of vibes, ARIA gets the assignment. Competitive notes, market scans, technical background, community sentiment, naming checks, SEO angles, and "what is actually out there?" questions all fit her lane.

ARIA does not decide strategy by herself. Her job is to bring back useful material so Caleb and Dani can make better calls.

### Current responsibilities

- Pull together sources before a post or product decision
- Check whether a tool idea already exists and where the gaps are
- Find community complaints that point to useful products
- Gather links, quotes, and background for Caleb to review
- Hand research back to Dani for synthesis and execution

---

### How she fits in

Research and execution are different modes. ARIA is intentionally not the general operator. She can spend a whole session on one question without dragging publishing tasks, repo state, or unrelated operational context along with her.

That separation keeps the crew useful. ARIA gathers. Dani turns the research into decisions, drafts, plans, or code tasks. Caleb makes the call.
